# Logo Routes

Use these as starting points for Vitrina sites.

## Carpenter / Woodworker

Symbols: wood planks, chisel, pencil, saw tooth, cabinet line, joinery corner.

Recommended composition:

- rounded square badge
- warm wood gradient or solid dark background
- two crossing/stacked wood forms
- small initials if readable

Avoid:

- complicated saw blades
- too many tools
- rustic clipart

## Restaurant / Tavern

Symbols: plate, flame, olive branch, table, fork/knife, cooking pot.

Recommended composition:

- circular or shield mark
- warm accent
- simple plate/flame line

## Cafe

Symbols: cup, steam, bean, small saucer.

Recommended composition:

- minimal cup line
- soft rounded badge
- one accent stroke

## Dentist / Medical

Symbols: tooth outline, smile arc, sparkle, clean cross.

Recommended composition:

- light/clinical palette
- simple tooth/smile line
- no scary dental tools

## Legal / Accounting

Symbols: columns, document, checkmark, scale, monogram.

Recommended composition:

- restrained wordmark
- serif or geometric mark
- dark blue/green palette

## Generic Local Business

Symbols: initials, location pin, storefront awning, simple monogram.

Recommended composition:

- initials inside geometric mark
- one local/business hint
