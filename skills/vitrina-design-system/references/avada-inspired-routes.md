# Avada-Inspired Route Notes

Use these notes as inspiration only. Do not copy Avada layouts, wording, imagery, or branding.

## What Avada Does Well

- Many prebuilt websites, grouped by business category.
- Each route has a clear commercial job: quote request, phone call, booking, portfolio browsing, shop/product discovery.
- Profession-specific navigation and sections instead of one generic landing page.
- Strong hero section with one obvious next action.
- Service grids, proof blocks, recent work, testimonials, and final CTA are repeated in different visual styles.

## Vitrina Routes For Local Greek SMBs

### Construction Pro

- Inspired by contractor/construction patterns.
- Best for: builders, carpenters, renovators, electricians, plumbers, serious local services.
- First screen: strong project photo, clear profession + city, two CTAs.
- Sections: services, recent work, numbers/proof, core values, contact.
- Tone: confident, grounded, professional.

### Handyman Call-first

- Inspired by phone-first handyman/plumber service pages.
- Best for: urgent or practical local services.
- First screen: phone, area, service promise.
- Sections: top services, why choose us, areas served, quote/contact block.
- Tone: direct, concrete, no fluff.

### Interior Portfolio

- Inspired by interior/furniture/portfolio pages.
- Best for: woodworkers, furniture makers, salons, designers, creatives.
- First screen: large crafted visual, elegant headline, portfolio CTA.
- Sections: image-led projects, services as collections, process/story, contact.
- Tone: premium, crafted, visual.

### Warm Local

- Inspired by friendly food/family business pages.
- Best for: tavernas, cafes, bakeries, neighborhood stores.
- First screen: warm hero image, locality, opening/contact.
- Sections: menu/products, story, reviews, map.
- Tone: welcoming and familiar.

### Minimal Clean

- Inspired by clean professional/clinic templates.
- Best for: dentists, accountants, consultants, clinics.
- First screen: calm promise, appointment CTA.
- Sections: services, trust points, process, FAQ, contact.
- Tone: calm, precise, trustworthy.
