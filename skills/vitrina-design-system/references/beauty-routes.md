# Beauty website routes

Use these three routes for nail studios, hair salons and beauty businesses.
They are references for structure and product thinking, not assets or pages to
copy. Every Vitrina output must use original composition, copy and branding.

## 1. Premium Editorial

- Reference: https://nea-nails.v0.build/
- Best for: nail studios, premium salons, beauty brands.
- Keep: art-directed full-bleed hero, expressive Greek serif typography,
  restrained coral accent, portfolio-led storytelling and premium booking CTA.
- Avoid: generic stock imagery, excessive monochrome and decorative animation
  that delays booking.
- Vitrina implementation: `beauty-atelier`.

## 2. Booking Studio

- Reference: https://rdsnailsandbeauty.ie/
- Best for: busy salons with many services and appointment slots.
- Keep: immediate Book Now action, service durations, clear treatment groups,
  reviews, opening hours and location information.
- Avoid: copying its brand, typography, imagery or exact layout.
- Vitrina implementation: build as a distinct service-and-booking-first route.

## 3. Social Beauty

- Reference: https://genevieve.ie/
- Best for: established local studios with reviews and active social media.
- Keep: real review score, Instagram proof, map, WhatsApp/phone and practical
  visit information.
- Avoid: its dated visual styling; retain the business proof in a cleaner,
  contemporary composition.
- Vitrina implementation: build as a distinct community-and-proof-first route.

## Shared acceptance criteria

- Greek-compatible, self-hosted fonts.
- Strong mobile booking action and no horizontal overflow.
- Original visual identity generated from the customer's profession and brand.
- Works with customer photos, curated licensed imagery or no photos.
- No fake reviews, ratings, results or unavailable services.
- Services, prices and durations come from customer data when available.
- Location, hours, phone and booking destination are visible and functional.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and maintain readable contrast.
