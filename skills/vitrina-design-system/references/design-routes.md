# Vitrina Design Routes

Use these routes as reusable art direction. They are not rigid templates; they are tasteful starting points.

## premium

- Best for: restaurants, salons, boutique services, lawyers, clinics.
- Feel: confident, editorial, polished, high-conversion.
- Layout: immersive hero, strong typography, premium image crop, proof row, elegant content bands.
- Palette: dark grounding color, warm accent, light panel, one muted secondary.
- Copy: short, sensory, specific.

## warm

- Best for: tavernas, cafes, family businesses, local stores.
- Feel: friendly, lived-in, familiar, welcoming.
- Layout: human-first hero, opening hours, menu/services, story, map.
- Palette: warm neutrals, terracotta/orange accent, green/sage or wine support.
- Copy: direct and hospitable.

## minimal

- Best for: dentists, accountants, consultants, clinics, modern cafes.
- Feel: clean, calm, trustworthy, easy to scan.
- Layout: clear hero, service tiles, trust points, appointment/contact section.
- Palette: white/off-white, calm blue/green, small accent.
- Copy: crisp, low-drama, professional.

## trust

- Best for: technicians, doctors, legal/accounting, urgent services.
- Feel: reliable, fast, practical.
- Layout: phone-first hero, badges, service grid, areas covered, reviews/proof, contact.
- Palette: navy/charcoal, orange or green CTA, light panels.
- Copy: concrete, action-oriented, no fluff.

## editorial

- Best for: businesses with strong photography or personality.
- Feel: magazine-like, crafted, memorable.
- Layout: big images, asymmetric story section, large section headlines, curated gallery.
- Palette: dark + off-white + one expressive accent.
- Copy: poetic but still useful.
