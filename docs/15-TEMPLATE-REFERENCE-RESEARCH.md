# Template Reference Research

Research date: 2026-08-06

## Why This Exists

Vitrina is not a theme marketplace. It is a managed website product for Greek
local businesses. The system should understand the profession and show each
customer three or four genuinely suitable directions, already filled with their
content, photographs, local SEO, phone, map, and primary action.

The current labels (`premium`, `warm`, `minimal`, `trust`, `editorial`) are useful
as art direction, but too broad as customer choices. They can easily produce five
pages with the same hero, service cards, gallery, and footer. The next design
system should be based on different business jobs and different page structures.

Use the references below for layout and interaction research only. Do not copy
their code, text, branding, or licensed imagery.

## The Ten Vitrina Families

These are the proposed core families. They describe structure and conversion,
not merely color or typography.

| Family | Structural signature | Primary action | Suitable professions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `project-canvas` | Full-bleed project hero, alternating case studies, material/detail gallery | Request a quote | Carpenter, kitchens, architect, construction |
| `urgent-local` | Phone-first hero, availability strip, service-area proof, compact service list | Call now | Plumber, electrician, locksmith, technician |
| `menu-house` | Venue/image hero, hours, visual menu, story, map and reservation band | Reserve / call | Taverna, restaurant, cafe |
| `product-window` | Product mosaic, categories, daily highlights, store details | Visit / order | Bakery, butcher, florist, local retail |
| `calm-clinic` | Reassuring hero, treatments, doctor/team, patient journey, FAQs | Book appointment | Dentist, doctor, physiotherapist |
| `beauty-booking` | Transformation imagery, service/pricing menu, team and booking rail | Book appointment | Salon, barber, nails, spa |
| `authority-journal` | Editorial masthead, expertise articles, practice areas, credentials | Consultation | Lawyer, accountant, notary, consultant |
| `proof-led-service` | Before/after, reviews, process and detailed service pages | Get estimate | Cleaning, renovation, painter, installer |
| `hospitality-story` | Human photography, local story, rooms/experience, reviews and directions | Book / enquire | Guesthouse, tourism, events |
| `compact-one-page` | Tight one-page flow with one dominant CTA and minimal navigation | Context-specific CTA | Solo professional, new business, low-content client |

## Recommendation Logic

Do not show five random templates. Select three or four families based on the
business and its strongest available material.

Examples:

- Carpenter with strong project photos: `project-canvas`, `proof-led-service`,
  `authority-journal`.
- Plumber with few photos: `urgent-local`, `proof-led-service`,
  `compact-one-page`.
- Taverna with food/space photos: `menu-house`, `hospitality-story`,
  `product-window`.
- Dentist: `calm-clinic`, `authority-journal`, `compact-one-page`.
- Hair salon: `beauty-booking`, `project-canvas`, `compact-one-page`.

Within a family, colors, font pairing, image crops and decorative details may
change. They must not be presented as separate designs unless the page structure
and customer journey also change.

## Quality Bar

Every proposed template must pass all of these checks:

1. The first mobile viewport identifies the business, city and primary action.
2. It looks intentionally designed for the profession, not recolored SaaS UI.
3. It works with real customer photography of mixed quality and aspect ratios.
4. It has useful Greek copy density without becoming a keyword dump.
5. Phone, map, hours, services and local area are easy to find.
6. Typography supports Greek characters and is self-hosted.
7. The layout remains stable at 360px, 768px, 1440px and wide desktop.
8. Motion is subtle and never blocks reading or calling.
9. Each service can support an SEO detail page later.
10. The design is original implementation inspired by patterns, not a clone.

## Recommended Next Build

Do not build twenty more templates immediately. Build three high-quality proofs
first and compare them with the current library:

1. `project-canvas` using Koutrakis and his real photographs.
2. `urgent-local` using a plumber/electrician demo with little photography.
3. `menu-house` using a taverna demo with menu and reservation content.

If these three clearly outperform the existing choices on desktop and mobile,
replace weak templates in the recommendation map. Keep the library compact and
curated even if old experiments remain in the repository.
