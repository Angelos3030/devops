# Koutrakis Demo Design Spec

## Route

Primary preview: `editorial` / Interior Portfolio.

Secondary previews: `premium` / Construction Pro and `trust` / Handyman Call-first.

## 1. Color

Grounding dark: warm charcoal for premium craft.
Accent: orange/wood tone for calls and highlight labels.
Light panel: warm off-white so the photos feel natural.
Secondary: muted green/grey for trust and local service cues.

## 2. Typography

Greek-safe modern sans stack: Inter / system UI.
Hero type is large but not decorative; service text stays compact and readable.
Avoid script or faux-handmade fonts because the photos already carry the craft feeling.

## 3. Components

Sticky nav, hero with real work photo, proof row, services grid, photo slideshow,
gallery, service areas, map/contact, final CTA, fixed mobile phone CTA.

## 4. Layout

Desktop uses a 1100-1200px content width with strong editorial image blocks.
Mobile is single-column with clear phone action and no horizontal overflow.
Services are dense enough for SEO without feeling like a keyword dump.

## 5. Motion

Restrained hover states and slideshow controls only.
No parallax, no heavy animation, no autoplay requirement for the owner demo.

## 6. Depth

Subtle shadows and 8px radii. Photos should feel framed, not trapped in heavy cards.

## 7. Do's / Don'ts

Do show Koutrakis as a real carpenter for kitchens, wardrobes, wood repairs and
custom furniture around Gerakas/Athens.
Do not invent years of experience, awards, reviews, or guarantees.
Do not start Facebook/Instagram promises until the Meta flow is ready.

## 8. Responsive

Check 360px, 768px and desktop. Fixed mobile CTA must not cover footer/contact content.
Images use real local assets from `assets/` and must keep meaningful alt text.

## 9. Accessibility

Single H1, Greek `lang`, phone links use `tel:`, visible focus states, meaningful alt text,
and no image-only actions.
